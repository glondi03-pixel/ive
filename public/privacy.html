Who is responsible for your data

For anything about your personal data, write to hello@ivepiano.com. As a one-person business we are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer; I handle privacy questions personally.

Ive is responsible for your data. Email me with any question.

Data we collect

When you use the contact form or email us: your name, email address, phone number (optional), preferred language, the subject of your message and whatever you write in it. Depending on the topic, also the age group and level of the student, or the date, place and size of an event.

When you or your child take lessons: name, contact details, for students under 18 the name and contact details of a parent or guardian, lesson schedule, attendance, notes about progress and repertoire, and invoice and payment details (amount, date, IBAN as shown on your bank transfer).

When you book a performance: the contact details of the client or organisation, event details, and invoice and payment details.

When you visit the website: our hosting provider automatically processes technical data needed to deliver the page — IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser type and referring page — in server logs. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

We do not ask for special categories of data (such as health). If you choose to tell us something like that — for instance a physical condition relevant to playing — we use it only to adapt the lessons, and only with your explicit consent.

Only what you send us, what's needed to teach and invoice, and basic server logs. No tracking.

Children

Under Dutch law, children under 16 need a parent or guardian's consent where processing is based on consent. Messages about lessons for a child under 16 should therefore be sent by a parent or guardian, and the lesson agreement for any student under 18 is entered into by a parent or guardian (see our terms). Photos or videos of a child under 16 are only made or published with the parent or guardian's consent.

For children, parents get in touch and decide about photos.

Transfers outside the EU

We prefer providers that store data within the European Economic Area. Where a provider processes data outside the EEA, this only happens on the basis of an adequacy decision of the European Commission (for instance the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, for certified companies) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with additional safeguards. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

Your data stays in Europe where possible, and is protected by EU-approved safeguards where it doesn't.

Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including an encrypted (HTTPS) connection for the whole website, two-factor authentication on email and administration accounts, encrypted devices, and access limited to the teacher and, for invoices, the accountant. If a data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we report it to the Dutch Data Protection Authority within 72 hours and inform you where required.

Encrypted, password-protected, and only seen by me.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data erased, unless we are legally required to keep it;
  • restrict the processing of your data;
  • receive your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format (data portability);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Send your request to hello@ivepiano.com. We respond within one month; this may be extended by two months for complex requests, in which case we tell you why. We may ask you to confirm your identity, but never ask for a copy of an identity document unless strictly necessary.

See, correct, delete or take your data with you. Ask by email; answer within a month.

Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, please let us know first. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority — in the Netherlands the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Dutch Data Protection Authority), Postbus 93374, 2509 AJ Den Haag, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl — or with the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

Talk to me first; you can always go to the Dutch privacy regulator.

No automated decisions or profiling

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, and we do not create profiles of website visitors or students.

A person — me — reads and decides everything.

Cookies

This website does not place cookies on visitors' devices or use tracking technologies, and loads no third-party content: fonts and images are served from our own server. The only cookie is a login cookie for the site owner's private editing area. Details are in the cookie policy.

No cookies for visitors. See the cookie policy.
