The teacher

I started playing the piano at the age of seven. What began as a hobby turned into a dream: combining a professional performing career with a private teaching studio.

I now study classical piano performance at the Conservatorium Maastricht. Among my recent teachers are Hanneke van Wijk, Anastasia Safonova, Ashley Wass and Jeroen Riemsdijk. I made my debut as a soloist at eighteen, playing Grieg's Piano Concerto with orchestra.

— Ive

I want to make a future in music accessible to everyone.

What my lessons include

About my lessons
  1. 01

    Stable piano technique

    Learn to play difficult passages while developing strong, independent fingers.

  2. 02

    Interpretation & musicality

    What does the composer mean, and what is the historical context? And how can we best bring that knowledge into our playing?

  3. 03

    Sound & expression

    How to create a beautiful sound and express all the different colours the music has to offer.

  4. 04

    Theory & analysis

    The theory behind the music: reading sheet music, analysing phrase structure and much more.
