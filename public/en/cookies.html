What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your computer, phone or tablet. Similar technologies — such as local storage, pixels and device fingerprinting — read or store information on your device in comparable ways. In this policy, “cookies” covers all of them.

The rules

Under article 11.7a of the Dutch Telecommunications Act (Telecommunicatiewet), which implements the EU ePrivacy Directive, a website may only store or read information on your device with your prior consent — unless it is strictly necessary to provide the service you ask for, or has little or no impact on your privacy. Where cookies process personal data, the GDPR applies as well.

Third-party content

All fonts, images and scripts are served from our own server, so visiting a page does not send your IP address to Google, Meta or any other third party. Links to other websites are ordinary links: nothing is loaded from those sites until you click, and once you are there their own cookie and privacy policies apply.

Should we ever embed videos or maps, they will only load after you click on them and give consent, and this policy will be updated first.

Why there is no cookie banner

A consent banner is only required when a website uses cookies that need consent. The only cookie on this website is the strictly necessary login cookie of the private editing area, which needs no consent and is never set for visitors — so there is nothing to ask you about.

Controlling cookies in your browser

You can view, block and delete cookies at any time in your browser settings — usually under “Privacy” or “Security”. Instructions are available on the help pages of Safari, Chrome, Firefox and Edge. Blocking cookies has no effect on this website.
