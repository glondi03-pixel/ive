Often asked

Do I need my own piano?

For weekly lessons, yes — but a good digital piano with weighted keys is perfectly fine for the first year or two. I'm happy to advise before you buy or rent.

Can parents sit in?

Always, for the first lessons. After that it depends on the child — some play better with an audience, some without. We'll decide together.

How far ahead should I book an event?

For weddings and festivals, three to six months. For house concerts and receptions a few weeks is often enough — just ask.
