How to change these texts — read this first

Step by step

  1. Keep a copy first. The editor only keeps the latest version. Before a bigger change, select all the text of the sections you will change and paste it into a document on your computer, with the date.
  2. Change the Dutch text, then the English one. Both versions must say the same thing. Switch with the Nederlands / English buttons above — they are saved together.
  3. Check the dates. “Last updated” changes to this month by itself. “In force from” you set yourself (see the rules below).
  4. Press Save & publish, wait a minute, then click View page and read the changed parts on the website — in both languages.
  5. Tell people when the rules say so (see below), by email to your current students and clients.

When the terms change

  • Changes that affect current students — prices, cancellation rules, how and when you invoice — may only start at least one month after you announce them by email. This is promised in the terms themselves (sections “Fees & payment” (Tarieven & betaling) and “Applicable law & changes” (Toepasselijk recht & wijzigingen)). Set “In force from” to that date.
  • Students who don't accept a change may stop their lessons from that date — that rule must stay in.
  • Changing prices: change them in the Fees tab. The terms don't mention amounts, so they don't need to change too, but the one-month notice above still applies.
  • Small fixes (a typo, clearer wording without changing what it means) need no announcement.

When the privacy policy must change

  • You start using a new service that handles people's data: a new email provider, a booking or payment system, a newsletter, an app or WhatsApp group for students, an accountant, a video-call service. Add it to “Who receives your data” (Wie je gegevens ontvangt), and to “Transfers outside the EU” (Doorgifte buiten de EU) if the company is outside the EU.
  • You collect new kinds of data (for example date of birth, recordings) or use data for something new: update “Data we collect” (Welke gegevens we verwerken), “Purposes and legal bases” (Doelen en grondslagen) and “How long we keep your data” (Hoe lang we je gegevens bewaren).
  • If a change affects data people already gave you, email your current students and clients (the policy promises this).

When the cookie policy must change

  • Only when something is added to the website that stores or reads information on visitors' devices: visitor statistics (Google Analytics and similar), embedded YouTube / Vimeo / Spotify / Google Maps, social-media buttons, chat widgets, newsletter sign-up boxes, online payment.
  • Important: most of those need the visitor's permission before they load, which means a cookie banner. That is a change to the website itself, not just to this text — ask a web developer, and update this policy and the privacy policy at the same time.
  • Changing concerts, photos, fees or texts in this editor never affects cookies.

What must always stay in

  • Terms: who you are (name, KvK number, address, email), prices & payment, cancelling, the 14-day right of withdrawal and the model withdrawal form (required for consumers who book online or by email), complaints, applicable law.
  • Privacy: who is responsible, which data and why, how long you keep it, who receives it, people's rights, and the right to complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
  • Cookies: what is stored on the visitor's device and why — today only the login cookie of this editor, which visitors never get.
  • Keep the {email}, {address}, {kvk} placeholders: they keep your details correct everywhere.

Good habits

  • Read all three texts once a year, for example every September before the new season.
  • Links to a section on another page (like [terms](terms.html#t-minors)) keep working when you rename a section. If you delete that section, check the pages that link to it.
  • Not sure whether a change is allowed or needed? Look it up on ondernemersplein.kvk.nl (terms) or autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl (privacy and cookies), or ask a lawyer before you publish.

Terms — check before saving: changes for current students start at least one month after you email them about it; set “In force from” to that date. Keep the right of withdrawal and the model form.

Privacy — check before saving: is every service that handles people's data listed under “Who receives your data” (Wie je gegevens ontvangt)? If the change affects data people already gave you, email them.

Cookies — check before saving: this text only needs to change when something new on the website stores information on visitors' devices. If that happens, a cookie banner is probably needed too — ask a web developer.
