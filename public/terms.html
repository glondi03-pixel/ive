Definitions

  • Teacher or we: the business described in article 1.
  • Student: the person who receives lessons.
  • Client: the person who enters into the agreement with us — the student, or the parent or guardian of a student under 18, or the person or organisation booking a performance.
  • Consumer: a client who is a natural person not acting in the course of a trade, business or profession.
  • Lesson: an individual piano lesson, in the studio or online.
  • Term: one of four teaching periods per season, as published on the website and following the Amsterdam (region North) school holidays.
  • Booking: an agreement for a live performance by the teacher.
The words we use, and what they mean.

Scope

These terms apply to every offer, agreement and lesson or performance provided by us. Deviations are only valid if agreed in writing (email is sufficient). General terms of the client do not apply.

If any provision of these terms is void or annulled, the remaining provisions stay in full force, and the parties will agree a replacement provision that is as close as possible to the original intent.

These terms cover all lessons and bookings, unless we agree something else by email.

Trial lesson

New students may take one free trial lesson of 30 minutes. A trial lesson creates no obligation for either party. The student or client decides afterwards whether to enrol.

The first half hour is free and you owe nothing afterwards.

The agreement

An agreement is concluded when we confirm the enrolment or booking in writing (by email), stating the type and length of the lessons, the fee and the start date. Because the agreement is usually concluded remotely, it is a distance contract within the meaning of Book 6, Title 5, Section 2B of the Dutch Civil Code.

Unless agreed otherwise, a lesson agreement runs for an indefinite period, starting on the agreed date. A ten-lesson card is valid for six months from the first lesson.

Nothing is binding until I confirm it to you by email.

Fees & payment

  1. Fees are as stated on the website or in the written confirmation, in euros. Prices for consumers include VAT where VAT applies.
  2. Lessons are invoiced per term in advance. Ten-lesson cards and conservatory-preparation packages are invoiced in advance in full.
  3. Invoices are payable within 14 days of the invoice date by bank transfer or by payment link.
  4. If payment is late, a consumer will receive a free written reminder giving a further 14 days to pay. If payment is still not made, statutory interest and extrajudicial collection costs may be charged in accordance with article 6:96 of the Dutch Civil Code and the Decree on Extrajudicial Collection Costs.
  5. We may change our fees once per season. Changes are announced at least one month in advance by email. A consumer who does not accept a change may end the agreement with effect from the date the change applies.
You pay per term, in advance, within 14 days. Price changes are announced a month ahead and you can stop if you don't agree.

Right of withdrawal

A consumer who concludes an agreement at a distance may withdraw from it without giving any reason within 14 days of the day the agreement is concluded. To withdraw, send us a clear statement — for instance by email or using the model form at the end of these terms — before the period ends.

If you expressly ask for lessons to start within the withdrawal period, and then withdraw, you pay a proportionate amount for the lessons already given up to the moment you inform us. Once all agreed lessons have been given in full at your express request, with your acknowledgement that you then lose the right of withdrawal, that right no longer applies.

We refund any payment you made, minus any proportionate amount owed, within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal, using the same payment method unless you agree otherwise.

The right of withdrawal does not apply to performance bookings for a specific date or period (leisure services for a specific date — article 6:230p of the Dutch Civil Code, implementing article 16(l) of Directive 2011/83/EU), nor to clients acting for a business.

If you sign up online or by email, you have 14 days to change your mind. Lessons you've already had are still paid for.

Cancelling and missing lessons

  1. A lesson cancelled by the student at least 24 hours in advance will be rescheduled within the same term, at no extra cost. Up to three lessons per season can be rescheduled this way.
  2. A lesson cancelled less than 24 hours in advance, or missed without notice, is charged in full and is not rescheduled — except in the case of sudden illness or an emergency, where we will try to find a replacement time.
  3. If we cancel a lesson, we offer a replacement lesson; if no suitable time can be found within the term, the lesson is credited or refunded.
  4. No lessons are given during school holidays and on Dutch public holidays. The number of lessons per season (36) already takes this into account.
Tell me 24 hours ahead and we move the lesson. Later than that, it's paid — unless you're suddenly ill.

Ending lessons

A consumer may terminate a lesson agreement at any time, with a notice period of one month, by email. Fees already paid for lessons that fall after the end date are refunded.

We may terminate a lesson agreement with one month's notice, or with immediate effect if the client seriously fails to meet their obligations — for example repeated non-payment after a reminder — or if continuing the lessons cannot reasonably be expected of us.

You can stop at any time with one month's notice, and get back what you've paid in advance.

Online lessons

Online lessons are given through a video-call service chosen by us. The student is responsible for their own device, internet connection and a suitable camera position. If a lesson cannot take place, or has to be cut short, because of a technical problem on our side, it is rescheduled; problems on the student's side are treated as a late cancellation.

Online lessons are not recorded by either party unless both agree beforehand.

Your connection is your responsibility; mine is mine. Nobody records without asking.

Students under 18

For students under 18, the agreement is entered into by a parent or legal guardian, who is responsible for payment and for communication about scheduling. Parents or guardians are welcome to attend lessons. Students under 12 must be collected from the studio by an adult unless agreed otherwise.

For children, a parent signs up, pays, and is always welcome in the room.

Teaching materials, photos & recordings

Practice plans, exercises and arrangements made by us remain our intellectual property. They may be used for the student's own study only and may not be published or shared commercially. Students buy their own sheet music; copied sheet music is only provided where the law allows.

Photos, audio or video of a student (for example at a student concert) are only made or published with prior consent — for students under 16, from a parent or guardian. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

My materials are for your practice only. I never post a photo or video of you without your permission.

Performance bookings

  1. For each booking we send a written confirmation stating the date, times, location, programme, fee, travel costs and any technical requirements. The booking is final once the client has confirmed it and paid a deposit of 30% of the fee.
  2. The balance is due no later than 7 days after the performance, unless agreed otherwise.
  3. The client provides a tuned acoustic piano or a suitable digital instrument as agreed, a safe and dry playing area, and access to the venue at least 60 minutes before the performance.
  4. If the client cancels: more than 60 days before the date, the deposit is retained; between 60 and 14 days, 50% of the fee is due; less than 14 days, the full fee is due. Costs already incurred (such as non-refundable travel) are always due.
  5. If we cannot perform due to illness or force majeure, we will make every effort to propose an equivalent replacement pianist, subject to the client's approval. If no replacement is accepted, all amounts paid are refunded in full.
  6. Recording, broadcasting or streaming a performance requires our prior written consent.
A booking is final once you've confirmed and paid 30%. Cancelling late costs more, the closer it gets to the date.

Liability

We carry out lessons and performances to the best of our ability. Our liability for damage resulting from an attributable failure is limited to the amount invoiced for the lesson period or booking concerned, and in any case to the amount paid out by our liability insurance, if any. We are not liable for indirect or consequential damage.

These limitations do not apply in the case of intent or gross negligence on our part, or where mandatory consumer law provides otherwise. We are not liable for loss of or damage to personal belongings brought to the studio, except through our own fault.

If something goes wrong through my fault, my liability is limited to what you paid — but never when the law says otherwise.

Force majeure

Neither party is liable for failing to perform if this is caused by circumstances beyond their reasonable control, such as serious illness, government measures, extreme weather or the closure of a venue. Lessons affected by force majeure are rescheduled or moved online where possible; otherwise they are refunded.

If something outside anyone's control stops a lesson or concert, we reschedule or refund.

See the privacy policy.

Complaints

Complaints about a lesson, an invoice or a booking should be sent by email to hello@ivepiano.com, describing the issue as clearly as possible, within a reasonable time after discovering it. We confirm receipt within 5 working days and give a substantive response within 14 days.

If a complaint cannot be resolved together, a consumer may submit the dispute to the competent Dutch court.

Email me. You'll hear back within two weeks.

We may amend these terms. Amendments are announced by email at least one month before they take effect and do not apply to bookings already confirmed. A consumer who does not accept an amendment may end the lesson agreement with effect from the date the amendment takes effect.

Dutch law applies. If the terms change, you're told a month ahead and can stop if you don't agree.
